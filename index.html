Iron Tide

MARINA DE GUERRA DEL PERÚ

Sink the enemy fleet before it sinks you. Each wave is bigger than the last.

  • W / S raise or lower engine speed
  • A / D turn the rudder
  • Mouse aim, click or Space to fire your main weapon
  • Click an enemy ship to select it (up to 3, generous click radius) — guns aim at your first selection, and E (missiles), T (torpedoes) or H (helicopter/aircraft) home in on everything selected; with nothing selected, missiles and torpedoes just fly straight and can miss
  • Press Shift to snorkel (faster, but can be detected at real range), press C to crash dive (slow, safe, runs on a limited battery) — submarine only
  • V active sonar ping (submarine only) — long reveal of ships at long range, but it also gives your position away
  • B opens the Tomahawk menu (submarine only) — a big sonar board where you click contacts to pick up to 2 targets, then press B again to launch long-range Tomahawks at them, even outside normal missile range. It gives away your position and those ships start hunting you
  • X deploys an acoustic decoy (submarine only) — any ship currently hunting you gets fooled into dropping depth charges on the decoy's position instead of yours
  • G launches a scout plane (carrier only) — temporarily extends your radar range
  • R damage control — a repair crew patches the hull, on a cooldown, for any ship

Passive sonar is short-ranged now — you'll need the active ping to really see what's out there, but it tells everyone in range where you are. Once a ship spots your submarine, it remembers you for a long while and can call nearby ships in on the search too — watch for a pulsing red ring on the sonar, that means someone has you. Crash dive is the only sure way to disappear completely. Torpedoes and missiles only truly home when you've selected a target first; fired blind, they travel straight and can miss a maneuvering ship. A carrier that takes heavy damage loses flight deck capacity. Land blocks everything, so use islands as cover.

PvP only — everyone who enters the same code fights each other in real time, no AI. Requires being signed in to this org or invited as a guest.
